BAO CAO CHUYEN MO HINH NHAN DIEN GIOI TINH TU KERAS SANG PYTORCH

1. Muc dich va du lieu

Notebook goc chuyen doi xu ly bai toan phan loai anh khuon mat thanh hai lop:
- Lop 0: female (nu).
- Lop 1: male (nam).

Anh dau vao la anh muc xam kich thuoc 64 x 64. Du lieu duoc luu trong bon tep NumPy:
- 64_64_11938_4098_train_x_onehot.npy: anh train, kich thuoc (11938, 64, 64, 1).
- 64_64_11938_4098_train_y_onehot.npy: nhan train one-hot, kich thuoc (11938, 2).
- 64_64_5968_4098_val_x_onehot.npy: anh validation, kich thuoc (5968, 64, 64, 1).
- 64_64_5968_4098_val_y_onehot.npy: nhan validation one-hot, kich thuoc (5968, 2).

Gia tri pixel nam trong khoang tu 0 den 255 va duoc giu nguyen nhu du lieu goc. Trong ban PyTorch, cac anh duoc chuyen sang dinh dang [N, 1, 64, 64], trong do N la kich thuoc batch, 1 la mot kenh anh muc xam, 64 va 64 la chieu cao va chieu rong. Nhan one-hot duoc chuyen thanh chi so lop bang argmax(axis=1), sau do thanh torch.long de su dung voi CrossEntropyLoss.

2. So sanh cac thanh phan Keras va PyTorch

Theo mo hinh va cac notebook da co trong du an BTL03, bang sau tong hop phep chuyen doi:

3. Chuyen kien truc CNN + MLP

Mo hinh PyTorch HumanGenderCNNMLP giu kich thuoc va so luong lop cua mo hinh Keras:

Khoi 1:
- Conv2d(1, 16), Conv2d(16, 16), MaxPool2d, Dropout 0.2.
- Khong gian anh giam tu 64 x 64 xuat 32 x 32.

Khoi 2:
- Ba Conv2d(32, 32), MaxPool2d, Dropout 0.2.
- Kich thuoc 32 x 32 giam con 16 x 16.

Khoi 3:
- Ba Conv2d(64, 64), MaxPool2d, Dropout 0.2.
- Kich thuoc 16 x 16 giam con 8 x 8.

Khoi 4:
- Ba Conv2d(128, 128), MaxPool2d, Dropout 0.2.
- Kich thuoc 8 x 8 giam con 4 x 4.

Khoi 5:
- Ba Conv2d(256, 256), MaxPool2d, Dropout 0.2.
- Kich thuoc 4 x 4 giam con 2 x 2.

Sau Flatten, tensor cuoi cung co:
256 * 2 * 2 = 1024 dac trung.
Lop nn.Linear(1024, 1024) ket hop ReLU va dropout.
Lop nn.Linear(1024, 1024) ket hop ReLU va dropout.
Lop cuoi nn.Linear(1024, 2) tra ve hai logits de phan loai female/male.

Mo hinh co 4.063.570 tham so co the hoc, khop voi tong tham so 4.063.570 trong mo hinh Keras goc.

4. Chuyen vong lap huan luyen

Trong Keras, model.compile() thiet lap optimizer, loss va metric. model.fit() thuc hien toan bo quyen cap nhat va ghi lich su huan luyen. Trong PyTorch, cac buoc nay duoc chia ra ro rang:

1. model.train() bat che do huan luyen va kich hoat Dropout.
2. optimizer.zero_grad(set_to_none=True) xoa gradient cu.
3. forward pass tao logits.
4. nn.CrossEntropyLoss tinh loss tu logits va chi so lop.
5. loss.backward() tinh gradient.
6. optimizer.step() cap nhat trong so.

Phan danh gia dung model.eval(), torch.inference_mode(), batch giam day du va khong tinh gradient. Moi bat luu so mau nhan dung, tong loss va tong so du doan dung. Cuoi moi epoch, loss va accuracy duoc tinh tren toan bo tap validation.

Ma huan luyen dung batch size 128, Adam learning rate 0.0001 va toi da 30 epoch. Validation dung batch size nho hon de giam tai bo nho CPU/GPU. Checkpoint co val_loss tot nhat duoc luu vao tep human_gender_best.pth.

5. Chuyen nhan va du doan

Voi nhan one-hot, vi du [1, 0] thanh female va [0, 1] thanh male sau khi argmax. Du doan mot anh khong dung softmax rieng trong forward. Mo hinh tra ve hai logits, argmax(dim=1) chon lop co diem cao nhat.

Vo i mot anh test:
- unsqueeze(0) chuyen anh tu [1, 64, 64] thanh [1, 1, 64, 64].
- model.eval() tat Dropout.
- torch.inference_mode() khong luu gradient.
- argmax(dim=1) lay chi so lop du doan.
- Ham int2gender chuyen chi so thanh chuoi female hoac male.

6. Ket qua huan luyen da co

Mot lan chay da hoan thanh 30 epoch tren CPU trong khoang 51,5542 phut. Cac chi so cua epoch tot nhat tren validation la:

Cac ket qua tren chi la ket qua cua mot lan chay cu the. De tai lap ket qua, can chay lai notebook voi cung seed va cung thiet lap phan cung. Seed hien tai la 42.

8. Cac diem can luu y

- Keras dung NHWC, trong khi PyTorch dung NCHW.
- CrossEntropyLoss phai nhan nhan chi so lop, khong duoc nhan nhan one-hot.
- Dropout chi hoat dong trong model.train(), khong hoat dong trong model.eval().
- Batch size danh gia nho ho tro giam tai bo nho.
- Trong moi truong Windows va Anaconda, mo phong loi xung dot OpenMP co the xay ra giua nhieu ban cua libiomp5md.dll. Notebook dat cac bien OMP_NUM_THREADS=2, MKL_NUM_THREADS=2 va KMP_DUPLICATE_LIB_OK=TRUE truoc khi import PyTorch/NumPy de giam xung dot kernel.
- Nen restart kernel truoc khi chay lai notebook tu dau de tranh giu lai ten bien, optimizer va bo nho tu lan chay truoc.
